SARB Banking Sector Credit Risk Analysis (2003–2007)

Tools: Python · SQL · Power BI · SQLite Context & Objective: Analysed 60 months of South African Reserve Bank BA Return regulatory submissions (DI500-Credit Risk, Total Banks) to assess credit risk trends, provisioning adequacy, and sectoral concentration in the South African banking sector between 2003 and 2007. How the Project Was Conducted: Built a Python parsing pipeline to extract and normalise six structured tables from raw regulatory CSVs into a SQLite database Wrote structured SQL queries covering NPL ratios, provision coverage, write-off trends, YoY loan book growth, sectoral concentration and geographic distribution Developed a three-page executive Power BI dashboard connected directly to the SQLite database Key Findings: Total banking sector gross exposure grew 279% from R4.8bn to R18.2bn over the period NPL ratio declined from 5.1% to 2.8% — driven by loan book expansion rather than genuine credit quality improvement Provision coverage fell from ~65% to ~35%, indicating the sector was structurally under-provisioned entering 2008 Individual borrowers represented a consistent ~40% concentration of total advances throughout the period

Power BI: Property Market Analysis

This Power BI analytics project assess historical property transaction data from 2013–2019, focusing on market value, sale price trends, redevelopment potential, and data completeness. It equips urban planners, investors, and policymakers with strategic insights into land use, pricing hotspots, and future investment zones. We followed a structured, insight-to-strategy approach:Phase Activity Executive Output Data Acquisition & Validation Imported and cleaned 2013–2019 transaction records, validated completeness across actuals and forecasts Reliable historical dataset Exploratory Analytics Mapped sale price trends, transaction volumes, and redevelopment index movements Identified market inflexion KPI. Definition & DAX Modelling Created metrics: CAGR, price per acre, land use pricing tiers, completeness % Strategic performance indicators. Key Findings Market Performance & Liquidity Price Contraction: The average sale price stands at R327,188, which is a significant drop from the R400k peak seen in 2016. Negative Growth: The market CAGR is -11.38%, underperforming significantly compared to broader benchmarks (like the S&P 500 average of +8.5%). Liquidity Crisis: There was a "sharp decline" in transactions driven by the COVID-19 pandemic, reaching record-low liquidity between 2018 and 2019. Land Use & Valuation Drivers Premium Locations: "Lincoya Creek Dr" is identified as the highest-value area by a wide margin, commanding R5.3k per acre. Asset Class Performance: Vacant Commercial Land is the highest-valued land use category (averaging R3.2M), followed by low-rise apartments. Light manufacturing and "split class" properties represent the lower end of the valuation spectrum. Redevelopment & Future Outlook Stable Redevelopment: Despite the price drop, the Redevelopment Index (percentage of parcels with activity permits) remains stable between 22% and 34%. Moving Average: The 3-year moving average for redevelopment shows a recovery trend starting after 2015 and maintaining a steady plateau through the 2020s. Strategic Recommendations Acquisition: Shift toward a phased strategy, targeting undervalued land while the market is in a contraction phase. Risk Mitigation: Avoid new capital deployment in Light Manufacturing due to high vacancy risks. Thesis: The stability of the Redevelopment Index supports a long-term land-banking approach, assuming the market eventually stabilizes above the R250k average price floor.

Power BI: Market Analysis

Context & Objectives The dashboard acts as a central business intelligence hub for a multinational retail chain (Maven Market) operating in the USA, Mexico, and Canada. Scope: An analysis of over 269,720 transactions from a customer base of 10,281, totaling $1.05M in profit. Primary Objective: To monitor real-time performance against monthly targets and identify the highest-value brands, customer segments, and geographic regions to optimize marketing and inventory. Historical Context: The data includes revenue trending from 1997 through late 1998, showing long-term growth patterns. Key Findings Monthly Performance vs. Goals Transaction Gap: Current month transactions (15,751) are significantly behind the goal of 113,788 (-86.16%), suggesting a major seasonal dip or a period early in the reporting cycle. Profit Variance: Monthly profit stands at $61.45K, trailing the $67.87K goal by 9.46%. Positive Returns: On a bright note, returns are at 414, which is 14.11% better (lower) than the goal, indicating high product satisfaction or effective quality control. Brand & Regional Dominance Top Brands: Hermanos is the leading brand by transaction volume (8,071), while Plato boasts the highest profit margin at 63.55%. Geographic Concentration: The USA is the dominant market by a significant margin, followed by Mexico and then Canada. This is mirrored in the "Country Transactions" treemap and the customer location bar chart. Revenue Growth: The "Weekly Revenue Trending" shows a steady upward trajectory from 1997 to 1998, with visible spikes in mid-to-late 1998. Customer Demographics & Behavior Gender Split: Revenue is nearly evenly split between genders, though females lead slightly in the "Bronze" and "Normal" membership categories. Income & Education: A large portion of revenue (38%) comes from a specific high-contributing demographic, with the "Revenue by Income" chart showing that the $30k–$70k income bracket (combined segments) represents a massive core of the business. Membership Value: Bronze member cards are the primary drivers of total profit, outperforming "Golden" and "Silver" tiers, likely due to a much larger volume of users in this tier. Critical Observations Return Rates: While the return rate goal is being met, certain brands like Horatio (1.25%) and Carlson (1.08%) show higher-than-average returns compared to the 0.99% portfolio average. Targeting: The business is heavily reliant on the North American market, specifically the West Coast of the USA and Central Mexico, as shown in the store transaction map.
